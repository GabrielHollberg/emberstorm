Inside your EmberStorm

You never need to open the box to use it. If you do, here is what each part is for, and how to keep your media safe.

The drives

Most boxes have two drives:

System drive

The small drive, labelled SYSTEM DRIVE. It holds EmberStorm's own system and nothing of yours.

Library drive

The large drive, labelled LIBRARY DRIVE. It holds your music, films, books and photos, and everybody's accounts and settings.

Your media is only on the library drive, not on both. Which slot each drive is in does not matter: EmberStorm recognises them by themselves.

Some boxes have one drive holding both, labelled SYSTEM AND LIBRARY: EmberStorm's system in its first 64 GB and your library in the rest, each in a part of its own.

Never plug the library drive (or a box's only drive) into a Windows computer. Windows cannot read it, and offers to "format" it - which erases everything on it. To get your media out, use one of the ways below instead.

If a drive is taken out of a two-drive box

  • Without the system drive, the box does not start. Nothing is lost: put it back and it starts as before.
  • Without the library drive, the box will not start EmberStorm. A monitor plugged into it says the storage drive is not responding and that nothing has been erased. Put it back and everything is as it was.
  • The box never starts afresh on its own because a drive is missing, so your family's accounts and media are never replaced by an empty box.

Getting your media out

  • In the app: open a song, album, film, book or photo, hold it (or right-click), and choose Download. A whole shelf downloads from Settings, Downloads.
  • Copying everything to a USB drive from the app, in a form any computer can read, is coming in an update.

Adding storage

The box has room to grow: a spare M.2 slot for an SSD (two, on a box with one drive), and two drive bays that take 2.5-inch or 3.5-inch SATA drives (hard drives or SSDs).

  • Using an added drive is coming in an update: when one is fitted, the app will ask whether it should add more space, or keep every file on two drives so one can fail without losing anything (protected).
  • Until then, a drive you add is left alone. Nothing on it is changed or erased.

Replacing a drive

  • The system drive (of a two-drive box): fit a new M.2 SSD of 128 to 512 GB in its place and start the box from an EmberStorm USB stick, which reinstalls the system. Your media, accounts and setup code are kept: they live on the library drive. Write to us for the stick.
  • The library drive: please write to us first, with the serial number from the back of the box. A new library drive starts empty, and the box will not take a new drive in place of the old one by itself - that is what keeps it from ever looking new while your real drive is only loose.
  • Reinstalling the system from the stick, on a box with one drive or two, rewrites only the system and keeps your library. Even so, copy your library to another drive first and unplug that drive: with a copy, nothing can be lost whatever happens. The stick reminds you before it starts.

Memory

The box's 16 GB of memory is built into the board. It cannot be added to or replaced, and it does not need to be: it is enough for everything EmberStorm runs.

Before opening the box

  1. Switch it off: press the power button once, or choose Switch off in the app (Settings, Devices). Wait until its light goes out.
  2. Unplug the power cable.
  3. Touch something metal to get rid of static before touching the drives.
  4. Leave the drive labels where they are, so whoever opens it next knows what each drive is.
