Set up your EmberStorm

  1. Plug it in

    Connect your EmberStorm to power and plug it into the internet. As soon as the device is powered on it will begin to configure itself!

  2. Install the app

    The EmberStorm app finds your server on its own! This may take a few minutes as the server sets itself up.
    • Android: from the releases page (the android ones).
    • iPhone: Try the iPhone app (beta), through Apple's TestFlight.
    You can also connect with a computer or tablet by opening emberstorm.local in your web browser.

  3. Scan the QR code

    If you installed the app you can scan the QR code on the back of your EmberStorm! Then choose your name and password - the box is yours.

If your device can't find the server

  • Make sure your phone or computer is on your home Wi-Fi, not a guest network or mobile data.
  • Give it a few more minutes the first time: Your EmberStorm server may still be getting everything ready.
  • If emberstorm.local doesn't open, plug a monitor into the box: its screen shows the web address to type and the setup code.
  • Lost the setup code? Plug your EmberStorm into a monitor to see the setup code on the screen! Or write to hello@emberstorm.app with the serial number from the box.
